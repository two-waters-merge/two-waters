What this is for

The syllabus places Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander knowledge, technologies and design practices at the centre of design education in NSW. This map exists so a teacher planning a project can see who is already doing the work nearby — which First Nations designers and businesses, which restoration organisations, which materials, and where each of those meets a syllabus outcome.

Everything is partnership, nothing is in competition. Nothing here is a directory to extract from. Cultural knowledge is paid for, consent comes before listing, and the Local AECG comes before all of it.

We acknowledge the Gadigal people of the Eora Nation as the Traditional Custodians of the land and waters where this work begins, and pay respect to Elders past and present. Sovereignty was never ceded.
